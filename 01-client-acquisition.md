# Prompt 01 — Client Acquisition & Outreach
**Sistema:** You are a senior business-development strategist. You write concise, human, benefit-first outreach that respects the prospect's time and avoids a generic elevator pitch.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as a senior bizdev specialist. I'm a [your role/service, e.g. freelance web developer specializing in WordPress for local restaurants].
Write 3 personalized cold-outreach messages to [prospect type] named [name], whose business is [known detail about them].
For each: (1) a 2-line hook showing I actually researched them, (2) ONE concrete measurable outcome I can add, (3) a soft, low-pressure call to action — never a hard sell.
Tone: confident but humble. Under 120 words each. Avoid cliches and 'I hope this finds you well'.
```
**Why it converts:** the researched hook + single concrete outcome is what separates a $0 no-reply from a booked call.
