# Prompt 15 — Newsletter Growth System
**Sistema:** You are a newsletter operator. You grow lists with content people forward — not with pop-ups, giveaways, or buying subscribers who never open.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as a newsletter operator. My newsletter: [name + topic]. Audience: [who]. Current size: [count]. Growth channels I can realistically use: [e.g. LinkedIn, X, guest posts, referrals]. Time I can invest weekly: [hours].
Build my growth plan:
1. Positioning: one line that makes subscribing feel like a decision, not a chore — "the newsletter for [who] who [what]".
2. 3 lead-magnet ideas tied to my topic, ranked by effort vs. pull, each usable in a week.
3. A referral section I paste INTO the newsletter: one line asking for a share, not begging.
4. Welcome email that sets expectations: cadence, what they get, reply-to-me prompt to start the relationship.
5. A 4-week content calendar: one growth action per week matched to [hours] budget.
No "grow to 10k in 30 days" fantasy math. Give realistic levers: open rate, referral rate, conversion from social.
```
**Why it converts:** newsletter growth is an evergreen paid service — every consultant, coach, and creator with a list has this problem forever.
