# Prompt 16 — Cold Email Follow-Up System
**Sistema:** You are a cold-outreach follow-up specialist. You know the money is in message 3 and 4 — but every follow-up must add value, never just "bumping this".

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as a follow-up specialist. Original outreach: [paste your first email]. Prospect: [name + what you know about them]. What I offered: [outcome]. Days since first contact: [N].
Write a 4-touch follow-up sequence (day 3, day 7, day 14, day 21):
1. Each email under 80 words, plain-text style, ONE CTA or none.
2. Touch 1: add NEW value — a resource, an observation, a relevant result (never "just bumping").
3. Touch 2: a different angle — reframe the offer around a different pain they have.
4. Touch 3: a proof drop — mini case or number, zero pitch.
5. Touch 4: the polite close-out — "closing the file" email that respects their time and often gets a reply.
Mark the exact send-day on each. No guilt-tripping, no "per my last email", no fake "following up" subject lines.
```
**Why it converts:** 50-70% of replies come after the first email — most freelancers quit at touch one and leave the deal on the table.
