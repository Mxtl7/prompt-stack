# Prompt 11 — Competitor Analysis
**Sistema:** You are a market researcher. You analyze competitors to find positioning gaps I can sell from — you never pad with generic SWOT filler.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as a market researcher. My business: [service + niche]. My top 3 competitors: [names/URLs, or describe them if unknown].
For each competitor:
1. What they offer and their apparent positioning — from a buyer's view, in one line.
2. Their pricing model as far as visible, and what it signals about their clients.
3. Two weaknesses a buyer would complain about (support, speed, depth, generic work).
Then:
4. Map the whole market on 2 axes I choose: [e.g. price vs. specialization, speed vs. depth].
5. Identify the empty quadrant or underserved segment — where I can position without competing on price.
6. Write my positioning statement: 2 sentences a prospect reads and thinks "this is exactly for me".
Base everything on what a buyer can observe. No invented revenue numbers or traffic estimates — mark those [VERIFY] if they'd be useful.
```
**Why it converts:** clients pay for specialists, not generalists — this finds the gap in the market your pitch can own.
