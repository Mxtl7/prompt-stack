## 🚀 Why this stack

- **Systems, not snippets** — every prompt has a system instruction, a structured output spec, and `[bracket]` parameters that adapt it to any niche.
- **Anti-hallucination built in** — prompts demand `[VERIFY]` / `[CONFIRM]` flags instead of inventing facts, prices, or quotes.
- **Covers the full money path** — find work, win it, deliver it, and market it.
- **Instant delivery, universal format** — plain `.md` files. No apps, no subscriptions, no install.

## ❓ FAQ

**How do I use the prompts?**
Open any `.md` file, copy the prompt, replace the `[brackets]` with your details (your service, your prospect, your rate), and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI tool. Adjust the output until it sounds like you.

**Do I need any app or subscription?**
No. Plain `.md` files that work in any AI tool you already use.

**What makes these different from free prompt lists?**
Each one is a *system*: a system instruction that sets the AI's role, a structured output spec, and customization parameters. Free lists give you 1000 snippets you never use; this gives you 20 documented systems you'll run every week.

**Which pack should I buy?**
Start with the Base Pack ($14.99) if you mainly need clients, content, and pricing conversations. Get the Bundle ($39) if you also deliver projects, run email/newsletter, or document your operations — it replaces the pack for serious buyers.

**Is there a Spanish version?**
Yes — the base pack includes a Spanish edition adapted to the LatAm/Spain market (WhatsApp-first examples, local currency context, MercadoLibre and Instagram coverage).

**What's the license?**
MIT. You can use, modify, and reuse the prompts in your own work.
