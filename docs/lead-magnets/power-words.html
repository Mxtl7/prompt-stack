4 rules so power words don't backfire

  1. Truth first. Urgency and scarcity only work if they're real. Fake "only 3 left" burns trust and can be illegal ad copy.
  2. One hero word per line. If everything screams, nothing does. Pick the single word that carries the idea.
  3. Number > adjective. "Grow fast" loses to "in 14 days." Specificity is the strongest word on this sheet.
  4. Say it out loud. If you'd never say the sentence to a client, cut it. Power words sharpen a true sentence — they don't replace one.
