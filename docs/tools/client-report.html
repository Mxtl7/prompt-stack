Client Report Generator

Fill in what you shipped this month, hit generate, and print a clean report with neutral branding — no vendor logos, no watermark. One click to PDF via your browser. Built for freelancers and small agencies who bill by the month.

1 Your details + client 2 Deliverables & metrics 3 Live preview 4 Print → Save as PDF

1 Who it's from & who it's for

These are the only identity fields in the report. Leave any blank and it's simply omitted from the output.

2 Deliverables

One row per thing you shipped or worked on. Status drives the colored label in the report.

3 Metrics

The numbers that prove the work — growth, reach, saves, revenue, hours saved. "Change" is free text (e.g. +18% MoM).

4 Next steps & notes

One next step per line — they become the report's bullet list.
