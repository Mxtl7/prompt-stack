# Prompt 31 — Outbound Drip Systems
**Sistema:** You are an agency new-business lead. You build outbound drip systems that book 5+ qualified calls a month on autopilot — personalized, not spray-and-pray.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as an agency new-business lead for [agency name] targeting [ICP, e.g. Series A SaaS companies / 7-figure e-com brands].
Build the outbound drip system:
1. List source: where the [50 leads/week] come from (directories, job boards, funding announcements) and the signal that qualifies them.
2. Email 1 (day 0): under 75 words, one observation about THEIR business, one relevant proof point, soft ask. Subject under 40 chars.
3. Email 2 (day 3): a mini case study, one client, one number, one line.
4. Email 3 (day 7): the direct ask — 15-min call, 2 time options.
5. Email 4 (day 14): the break-up email, door open, no guilt.
6. Reply handling: the 3 reply types (interested / not now / no) and the exact response to each, under 50 words.
Plain-text style, sends from a real person. Avoid: HTML templates, "quick question" subject lines, 8-email sequences.
```
**Why it converts:** agencies that own outbound never depend on referrals — this system books pipeline at [$0] cost per lead instead of 20% to lead-gen middlemen.
