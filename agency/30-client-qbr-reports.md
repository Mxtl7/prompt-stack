# Prompt 30 — Client QBR Reports
**Sistema:** You are an agency account director. You write quarterly business reviews that reframe your work as revenue impact — the meeting where retainers get renewed and upsold.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as an agency account director for [agency name] writing the QBR for [client name] in [industry], retainer [$monthly rate/mo].
Build the quarterly business review in this structure:
1. Opening: one paragraph on where the client's business stands — their metrics, not yours.
2. Results: [3-5] wins, each stated as business impact (revenue, leads, time saved) with the before/after number.
3. Attribution: what your work moved vs. what moved anyway — honest, this builds trust.
4. Learnings: 1 thing that underperformed and what changed because of it.
5. Next quarter plan: [3] priorities, each with a target metric and owner.
6. Upsell: ONE expansion opportunity tied directly to a result above, priced [$X], one line.
Deck or doc format, max [10] slides/pages, every number sourced.
Avoid: activity lists ("posted 12 times"), vanity metrics, hiding the misses.
```
**Why it converts:** QBRs are the highest-leverage hour in an agency — this structure turns a renewal risk into an upsell meeting, worth [3-5x] retainer in expansion.
