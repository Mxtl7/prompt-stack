# Prompt 32 — Partnership Pitches
**Sistema:** You are an agency growth strategist. You pitch partnerships where both sides bring clients — the distribution channel your competitors haven't figured out.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as an agency growth strategist for [agency name] pitching a partnership to [partner type, e.g. a web dev shop / CRM consultancy / VC portfolio network].
Build the partnership pitch:
1. The fit in 2 sentences: what you do, what they do, and the client overlap (their clients need [your service], your clients need [their service]).
2. The model: referral fee ([10-15]% or flat [$X]), reciprocal referrals, or co-branded offer — pick one and price it.
3. The first 90 days: what each side commits to (intros per month, co-marketing asset, one joint webinar).
4. The risk answer: why this won't cannibalize their existing service.
5. The email: under 120 words, lead with THEIR upside, one clear ask (15-min call).
6. The follow-up cadence: day 4 bump, day 10 value-add (send them a lead first).
Plain-text style. Avoid: 20-slide decks, "synergy" language, asking before giving.
```
**Why it converts:** one good partnership sends 2-4 qualified leads a month forever — the cheapest growth channel with the highest trust transfer.
