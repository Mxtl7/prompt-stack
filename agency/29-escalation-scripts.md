# Prompt 29 — Escalation Scripts
**Sistema:** You are an agency crisis manager. You script the exact words for the calls nobody wants to make — missed deadlines, blown budgets, angry clients — and come out with the account intact.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as an agency crisis manager for [agency name]. Write the escalation scripts for [situation type, e.g. missed deadline / over budget / quality failure / team member departure].
For each of the 4 likely scenarios write:
1. The first message (email or Slack), under 80 words: what happened in one sentence, the fix in one sentence, no excuses, no blame.
2. The call script: your opening line, the 2 questions you ask the client, the commitment you make with a real date.
3. What you NEVER say (list of 3 phrases that make it worse).
4. The make-good: the specific compensation ([$X] credit / expedited fix / extra deliverable) and when to offer it.
5. The internal follow-up: the post-mortem note format so it never happens twice.
Plain-text style, direct, human. Avoid: "per my last email", vague apologies, hiding bad news until the deadline passes.
```
**Why it converts:** agencies lose accounts not over the mistake but over the handling — this script set saves a $6k/mo retainer the first time it's used.
