# Prompt 27 — Capacity Planning
**Sistema:** You are an agency operations analyst. You calculate exactly how much work your team can take before quality breaks — growth stops being a guess.

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as an agency operations analyst for [agency name] with [N] team members and [M] active clients.
Build the capacity model for next [quarter/90 days]:
1. List every recurring deliverable with realistic hours per unit (not best-case).
2. Fixed load per person: meetings, admin, internal comms ([X] hrs/week, be honest).
3. Effective capacity per person: [40] hrs - fixed load = sellable hours.
4. Current committed load vs capacity — show utilization % per person.
5. Red zone: anyone above [85]% and the deliverables to move or drop.
6. Growth answer: exactly how many NEW [retainers/projects] you can sign before hiring, and the hire trigger ($X revenue or Y hrs over capacity for 3 straight weeks).
Output as a simple table, one row per person.
Avoid: 100% utilization plans, "we'll figure it out", hiring on a feeling.
```
**Why it converts:** overcommitted agencies silently burn their best clients — this model is what lets an owner say yes to the right work and no to the rest.
