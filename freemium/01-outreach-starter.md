# Free Prompt 01 — Cold Outreach Starter
From "The Modern Freelancer's AI Prompt Stack" — free mini pack (1 of 3)

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as a business-development specialist. I'm a [your service, e.g. freelance web developer for local restaurants].
Write ONE cold-outreach message to [prospect type] whose business has [one detail you know about them].
Include: (1) a one-line hook that proves I actually looked them up, (2) one concrete outcome I could deliver for them, (3) a low-pressure question that invites a reply — never a hard sell.
Under 100 words. No cliches, no "I hope this finds you well".
```

**What it does:** turns a generic cold email into a researched, benefit-first message worth replying to.
