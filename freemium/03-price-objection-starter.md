# Free Prompt 03 — Price Objection Starter
From "The Modern Freelancer's AI Prompt Stack" — free mini pack (3 of 3)

## The prompt (fill the [brackets]):
```
Act as my negotiation coach. A prospect wants [service] but their budget is [budget] and my rate is [rate].
Give me 2 short responses that defend my value without dropping my core price (offer a scope trim or payment terms instead).
Keep it human and direct — not pushy.
```

**What it does:** gives you ready words for the moment a prospect pushes on price, so you trade scope instead of caving on rate.
